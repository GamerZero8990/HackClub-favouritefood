It´s so tasty because it´s really hot and you can use all the topings you want A pizza (pronounced [ ˈpɪtsa ], Italian [ ˈpitːsa ], German plural : die Pizzas or die Pizzen [ 1 ] ) is a flatbread made from simple yeast dough , topped with savory ingredients before baking, originating in Italian cuisine . The modern, internationally widespread version with tomato sauce and cheese as a base is thought to have originated in Naples . In 2017, the Neapolitan art of pizza making ( the art of Neapolitan 'Pizzaiuolo' ) was inscribed on UNESCO's list of Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity . [ 2 ] Along with spaghetti, pizza is now the most famous Italian national dish ; it is served worldwide.
